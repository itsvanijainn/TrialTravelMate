About TravelMate 💌

TravelMate was born from a simple love of crumpled train tickets, pressed wildflowers inside pocket journals, and the magic of pastel memories.

🌸 The Scrapbook Philosophy

We believe travel planning shouldn't feel like an Excel spreadsheet or a cold boardroom schedule. It should feel like opening a treasured diary filled with stickers, handwritten postcards, and excitement for every bite and breeze.

✨ What Makes It Special

Fully offline-friendly, zero clutter, zero invasive trackers. Just pure, delightful craft: day-by-day morning-afternoon-evening layouts, intuitive packing tallies, and gentle currency budgeting.

🎨
Crafted with love for explorers, dreamers & scrapbookers.
